HTTP STATUS CODES

10 every dev should know
2xx worked 3xx look elsewhere 4xx your fault 5xx server's fault
  1. 200

    OK

    it worked, here you go

  2. 201

    Created

    a new item was saved

  3. 301

    Moved Permanently

    it lives at a new URL

  4. 304

    Not Modified

    use your cached copy

  5. 401

    Unauthorized

    who are you? log in

  6. 403

    Forbidden

    I know you. Still no.

  7. 404

    Not Found

    nothing lives here

  8. 429

    Too Many Requests

    slow down, try later

  9. 500

    Internal Server Error

    the server broke, not you

  10. 503

    Service Unavailable

    too busy, come back soon
